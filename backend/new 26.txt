Frankly speaking, I never imagined putting myself on a public platform as an astrologer. For years, I preferred working quietly within a close-knit circle of family, friends, referrals, and a handful of clients. My focus was always on helping people, not on building a public profile.

Recently, however, Dipali encouraged me to participate in an event, and it made me pause and reflect.

Over the past few years, I have witnessed many genuine stories where my astrological reports and guidance have helped people gain clarity during important phases of their lives. Those experiences gradually changed my perspective. I realized that if my work is consistently helping people, perhaps it should reach a wider audience.
One incident, in particular, stayed with me.

During my nephew's wedding, I happened to meet a physician from Denver. While casually discussing his chart, I pointed out a specific period during the wedding festivities when health-related concerns could arise and suggested a few precautions. At the time, he was polite but clearly skeptical. As a doctor, he found it difficult to believe that astrology could indicate something so specific.
The wedding ended, life moved on, and I completely forgot about the conversation. In fact, I had spoken to nearly forty people during those few days and didn't even remember his face.
About a month later, I received a phone call from him. He introduced himself and reminded me of our conversation. He told me that the health issue had unfolded almost exactly as I had described. He admitted that, being a doctor, he had not believed me initially, but the accuracy of what happened made him rethink his assumptions. He then asked if I would do a complete analysis for him.
He flew from Denver to Atlanta for a single day just for that consultation.
At the end of our session, he told me something I will never forget. He said that many people had looked at his chart before, but no one had explained his life, his challenges, and the practical path forward the way I had.

There is no amount of money that can equal the satisfaction of hearing those words. Knowing that someone leaves with greater clarity and hope is far more rewarding than any fee.
Another experience reinforced that feeling.

A group of friends had gathered one evening, and one of them brought along a friend who openly admitted he did not believe in astrology or anything related to it. Out of curiosity, he challenged me to read his chart.
We began around 10:00 at night.

The discussion continued until nearly 4:00 the following morning.
For six hours, I analyzed his chart, explained different phases of his life, discussed his personality, relationships, career, and the reasoning behind each observation. Throughout the entire conversation, he hardly interrupted. Although we had never met before, he later told me that it felt as though I had known him personally for years because the observations were so precise.
Whether he became a believer is secondary. What mattered was that he left with a completely different perspective and a genuine openness to understanding astrology.
There have been many similar experiences. Former college friends have reached out when they needed guidance. Families have referred their loved ones. Clients from different states, and even from outside the country through online consultations, have come almost entirely through word of mouth.

Every one of these experiences has reinforced the same lesson.
People are not looking for fear.
They are looking for clarity.
That is what I want ORBINOVASTRO to represent.

I believe astrology should never frighten people or make them feel powerless. Its purpose is to increase awareness, provide perspective, and help people make wiser decisions. A birth chart reveals tendencies and timing, but it also reveals opportunities for growth, preparation, and conscious action.
There are many knowledgeable astrologers, and I have deep respect for the wisdom they have preserved. My goal is not to claim superiority over anyone. My aspiration is simply to contribute in my own way by combining traditional astrological principles with careful research, structured analysis, and practical guidance that people can understand and apply in their everyday lives.

Today, I genuinely believe I have something meaningful to offer. If my work can bring even a little more clarity, confidence, and peace into someone's life, then perhaps it is time to step beyond my close circle and share it with a wider audience.
If ORBINOVASTRO becomes known for anything, I hope it is this:
Not astrology that predicts fear, but astrology that inspires understanding.
Not astrology that creates dependence, but astrology that empowers better decisions.
Not astrology that takes away hope, but astrology that helps people find their path with greater awareness.
